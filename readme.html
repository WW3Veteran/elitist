The Product

Everyone knows the name of the game. Brave the endless coffee chats, the application, multi-round interviews (run by people no more than two years older than you) to get into the right club, work your way up to Executive Board by any means, network at every turn, and soon you'll be looking at an offer letter from the company of your choice. The not-so-easy part is choosing which club to spend 10 hours a week for the next 4 years.

At its heart, Elitist is a centralized database that tracks where alumni from Cornell's engineering and finance student organizations end up working at. Normally, all this information is scattered across Reddit posts, word of mouth intel during Clubfest, and self-congratulatory club info sessions.

The Data & Methodology

Placement data and number of alumni tracked are sourced using the alumni tool from LinkedIn, which can be accessed by going to Cornell's page. Placement numbers were obtained using the [Club Name] + [Where they currently work] search query. Data breakdowns by major and class year as well as the current member count for each club was obtained from the club's website itself, which sometimes listed members' major and grad year. In edge cases where people double majored, both their majors would be counted, which sometimes means that the total number in the major breakdown can slightly exceed the current member count.
